Changelog

Built in public, shipped often

Every release that changes what the app does, documented here. Rule-engine changes get special care — practice is only honest if the rules behave exactly as documented.

1.0 — Launch · SEPTEMBER 2026 · iOS & ANDROID

The first public release: everything needed to rehearse a prop-firm evaluation end to end.

  • Evaluation engine — one-step and two-step challenges on 5K–200K virtual accounts; profit targets, daily loss limits, static and trailing drawdown, minimum trading days and consistency rules, all measured against equity including open positions, tick by tick.
  • Editable rule profiles — match the firm you're targeting, down to the timezone of the daily reset.
  • Live markets — real prices across forex, metals, indices and crypto, with market hours respected, realistic spreads, and slippage modelled. A strict quote pipeline refuses to trade on stale prices.
  • Risk Guard — pre-trade warnings when a stop-out on the order you're about to place would breach a rule, with the position size that wouldn't.
  • Mistake engine — 28 detectors (revenge entries, oversizing, missing stops, cut winners, and more), each priced in dollars, with a process grade out of 100 per trade.
  • Challenge autopsy — when a run ends, the full story: the trade that ended it, the sequence behind it, and what surviving would have required.
  • Readiness estimate — a Monte Carlo pass-probability simulated from your own recent trades, with the single change that would raise it most.
  • Simulated funded stage — pass a full evaluation and graduate to a clearly-labelled simulated funded account with a shareable certificate.
  • Courses — structured lessons tied to what the coach actually sees in your trading.
  • Pricing — generous free tier (full simulator, one complete evaluation), one-off attempt packs, and Pro for unlimited runs and full analysis.

Pre-launch betas · SUMMER 2026

Highlights from the beta period, for the curious:

  • Market-price pipeline hardening — strict quote contracts, restart recovery, and stale-price waivers so the simulator never fills orders on prices the market didn't print.
  • Mistake Lab — the detector suite grew to 28, learned rule profiles and scope-vs-period accuracy, and gained regression tests against fixture trading sessions.
  • Design system — the app moved to a clean, light-first broker aesthetic with full dark-mode parity; this site's Volt Noir / Ink & Lime theme is its public face.
Coming next. On the roadmap: server-side practice tooling (alerts and order management that survive your phone being off), more instruments, and deeper per-firm rule templates. Follow @fundedlot for release notes as they ship.
