Why practice first

Broker demos don't enforce the rules. Prop firms don't let you rehearse them.

That gap is where most fees are lost. A demo never breaches you for a 5% down day — so the first time you feel a real daily loss limit is the day it takes your money.

Questions

Asked often, answered straight

Is FundedLot a prop firm?

No. FundedLot is an educational trading simulator. Everything inside the app runs on virtual funds — we are not a broker, we don't offer real-money trading, and we don't sell funded accounts or pay out profits. What we do is let you rehearse the exact rules prop firms use, so a real attempt isn't your first attempt.

Is it really free?

The simulator, real market prices, all instruments, and your first full evaluation are free, with no card required. Paid attempt packs and the Pro subscription exist for people who want unlimited evaluations and the deeper analysis suite.

Are the prices and rules realistic?

Prices are real market quotes across forex, metals, indices and crypto, with realistic spreads and slippage. Rules are enforced the way real evaluations enforce them — daily loss checked against equity including open positions, drawdown tracked tick by tick, breaches final.

Will passing a FundedLot evaluation get me a funded account?

No — and be wary of anyone who promises that. Passing on FundedLot earns a simulated funded stage and a shareable certificate. The real value is arriving at a paid evaluation already knowing the rules, your risk numbers, and your failure patterns.

Which platforms does it run on?

FundedLot is built for iOS and Android. The Android app is live on Google Play now; the iPhone version is on the way. Get it from the download page.

Is this financial advice?

No. FundedLot is for education and practice. Nothing in the app or on this site is investment advice, and simulated results never guarantee real ones. Read our risk disclosure.
