Free signal questions

Are these trading signals really free?

The record is: every AI signal with its entry, stop, targets and result, once it closes. Live signals as they post, sized to your account, come with FundedLot Mentorship in the app.

Why not give live signals for free?

Because a live signal is only useful with the right size for your account and your rules. Members get both, a mentor and the AI coach; everyone gets the full results to judge the signals on.

How do I know the results are real?

Each AI signal posts at the live price before the move. A tracker follows it on 1-minute bars until a target, the stop or a time limit ends it, and the result goes on the record with the time. Losses stay next to the wins.

Which markets are covered?

EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, gold, silver, the Dow, the Nasdaq 100, the S&P 500, Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana.
