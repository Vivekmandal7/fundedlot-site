Questions

Free practice FAQ

Is prop firm practice on FundedLot really free?

Yes. The simulator, real market prices, all instruments and one full evaluation cost $0, with no card required. The free tier is ad-supported; paid tiers remove ads and add unlimited evaluations and deeper analysis.

Why is it free — what do you get out of it?

The honest answer: ads on the free tier, and a share of users who want unlimited runs and full analysis upgrading to packs or Pro. We sell practice depth. We never sell funded accounts, signals, or promises.

Can I practise a specific firm's challenge for free?

Yes — rule profiles are editable on every tier. Copy the target, daily loss, drawdown style and minimum days from the firm's published rules and run your free evaluation on those exact settings.

Do free evaluations use real market prices?

Yes. Free and paid tiers share the same live price pipeline across forex, metals, indices and crypto. A rehearsal on fake prices would be theatre.

What happens when my free evaluation ends?

You get the full result either way — pass or a complete failure autopsy. To run another evaluation you can buy a one-off attempt pack from $4.99 or go unlimited with Pro. Your history, grades and mistake data stay with you.
