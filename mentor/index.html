Plain answers

Mentorship questions

What does FundedLot Mentorship include?

Live signals with an entry, a stop and three targets, sized to your account and your firm's rules; a mentor 1:1 in chat with a monthly review; the pass tracker; the AI coach; the full Mistake Lab with the Risk Guard on every order; and no ads. One membership, monthly or yearly.

How much does it cost, and where do I pay?

The price shows in the app, in your own currency, set by the App Store or Google Play. You pay there, monthly or yearly; the website takes no payments. Cancel any time from your store's subscriptions, and access runs to the end of the paid period.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Follow the plan for the guarantee period: take the signals at the size the app gives you, stay inside your firm's rules on the account you told us about, and do your monthly review with your mentor. If you have not passed by then, ask for your money back in the app. We check the conditions and answer within 7 days.

Do I need Telegram?

No. Every signal and update reaches the app with a notification. The Telegram room adds the signals as they post and the members' group; the app gives members a one-time invite.

Can I fill my plan on the website?

Yes. Sign in on fundedlot.com with the email you use in the app, fill your plan, and the app reads it. You can change it any time, here or in the app.

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are educational market analysis. You decide whether to trade, where and at what size, on your own account. Nothing here promises funding, payouts or profit, and trading involves substantial risk of loss.
