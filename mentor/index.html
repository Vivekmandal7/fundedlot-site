How it works

Setups you decide on. A reviewer who is not you.

Prop firms forbid copying a third party's trades. So the room never posts a price to mirror: it posts the analysis, and you make the trade — or skip it — on your own account, at your own size.

Plain answers

Mentor questions

Is this a signal service?

No. The room posts setups: an instrument, a bias, an entry zone, an invalidation level, a target and the reasoning. You decide whether to trade it, where in the zone, and at what size, on your own account. Prop firms forbid copying another party's trades and “group trading”; a room that posts prices to mirror puts its members' accounts at risk. Ours does not.

What is the pass rate?

We show the count of verified passes next to the number of members it came from and the month the room opened, and we leave it at that. A pass is a certificate a member uploaded in the app and consented to show, checked by hand. We do not quote a rate without its denominator, and no trader's pass predicts yours.

How do I join?

In the app: Coach → Mentor → Check my fit. Five taps — your firm, your phase, your size, your session, what keeps going wrong — then the plan that fits, as a subscription through the App Store or Google Play. After that, Join the room: the app gives you a single-use link and the room's bot lets you in.

Do I need Telegram?

No. Every setup and outcome is also in the app's Mentor hub with a push notification. Telegram adds the members' group and, on Mentor 1:1, the direct line to your mentor. When a subscription ends, the bot removes you after a short grace period, automatically.

Can I cancel? How are refunds handled?

Yes, any time, from your App Store or Google Play subscriptions: access runs to the end of the paid period. Refunds follow the store's refund policy; see our terms of service.

Which markets and sessions?

Forex majors, gold and the main indices, in the London and New York sessions, two to four setups a day. The figures on this page come from the room's current settings at the last site build.

What if Mentor 1:1 is full?

Seats are capped per mentor so the weekly review stays real. When a mentor is full, the plan shows a waitlist in the app; Circle is open meanwhile, and a seat opens as soon as one does.

Is this financial advice?

No. Setups are educational analysis of market structure. Nothing on this page or in the room is a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument, and nothing here is a promise of funding, payouts or profit. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.
