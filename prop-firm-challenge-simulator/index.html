Prop firm challenge simulator

A challenge simulator is only useful if it can fail you

Anyone can plot prices on a chart. A real prop-firm challenge simulator has to enforce the rulebook — daily loss limits, drawdown floors, profit targets, minimum days — exactly the way a paid evaluation would. That enforcement is the entire product.

What "simulator" has to mean

Five things a challenge simulator must get right

If any of these are missing, you're using a demo account with a costume on. FundedLot was built around all five.

1 · Rules enforced tick by tick

Daily loss and drawdown are checked against equity including open positions, not just closed balance — the measurement that actually breaches real accounts. A floating loss can end the evaluation before you ever close the trade.

2 · Breaches that are final

Cross the limit and the evaluation fails. No undo, no "just this once." The consequence is the lesson — that's what a broker demo can never teach you.

3 · Real market prices

Live quotes across forex, metals, indices and crypto, with market hours respected, realistic spreads, and slippage modelled. Rule pressure only feels real when the prices are.

4 · Editable rule profiles

Targets, daily loss, static or trailing drawdown, minimum trading days, consistency caps, even the timezone of the daily reset — configured to match the firm you're actually targeting.

5 · An autopsy after every failure

The trade that ended it, the sequence behind it, and what surviving would have required. A simulator that fails you without explaining is a slot machine.

The honest limit

No simulator reproduces the feeling of real money — nothing does, at any price. What it reproduces is the rulebook, so the rules are boring before your fee is on the line.

Questions

Challenge simulator FAQ

What is a prop firm challenge simulator?

It's a practice environment that recreates a prop-firm evaluation end to end: virtual capital, real market prices, and the full rulebook — profit target, daily loss limit, maximum drawdown, minimum trading days — enforced automatically. Break a rule and the simulated evaluation fails, exactly as a paid one would. The point is to rehearse the rules before a real attempt costs you a fee.

Is a simulator the same as a demo account?

No. A demo account gives you prices with no rules — you can lose 20% in a day and keep trading. A challenge simulator adds the constraint system that actually decides evaluations. Roughly speaking: demo teaches the platform, the simulator teaches the exam.

Can I match the rules of the specific firm I'm targeting?

Yes. Rule profiles in FundedLot are editable: one-step or two-step, target percentages, daily loss method, static or trailing drawdown, minimum trading days, consistency rules, and the reset timezone. Copy the numbers from the firm's help pages and rehearse those exact settings.

Will passing in a simulator guarantee I pass a real challenge?

No, and distrust anyone who says otherwise. What it changes is the failure modes: rule breaches from sizing errors, revenge trades and limit misunderstandings are rehearsable, and those are what end most first attempts. The market risk is still yours.

How much does it cost?

FundedLot is free to start — full simulator, real prices, and your first complete evaluation included, no card required. Attempt packs and Pro exist for unlimited runs and the deeper analysis.
