Privacy Policy

Effective 27 August 2026. Applies to the FundedLot mobile app and fundedlot.com.

The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run a practice app — an email if you create an account, your practice activity, purchase records from the app stores, and standard crash and usage analytics. We don't sell your data, we don't collect financial credentials, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Who we are

FundedLot ("FundedLot", "we", "us") operates the FundedLot mobile application and the website fundedlot.com (together, the "Service"). For any privacy question or request, contact support@fundedlot.com.

2. What we collect

Information you provide.

  • Account details — if you create an account: email address, display name, and authentication identifiers. You can use core practice features without an account.
  • Support messages — emails you send us, including any attachments you choose to include.

Information created by using the app.

  • Practice activity — your simulated trades, evaluations, journal entries, settings and coach results. This is simulation data on virtual funds; it is not real financial account data.
  • Purchase records — when you buy an attempt pack or subscription, we receive transaction confirmations (product, time, status) from Apple, Google, and our purchase-management provider. We never see or store your card number.
  • Device & usage data — device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash reports, and anonymized usage events (e.g., which screens are used), used to fix bugs and improve the app.

What we do not collect: real brokerage or bank credentials, government identification, precise location, or your contacts. The app has no real-money trading, so it never asks for payment details outside the app stores' own billing systems.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: run simulations, sync your data across devices when signed in, and restore purchases.
  • To operate coaching features: your practice history is analyzed inside the Service to produce mistake reports, grades and readiness estimates for you.
  • To fix and improve: crash diagnostics and aggregated usage patterns.
  • To communicate: service messages (e.g., purchase receipts, important changes) and replies to your support requests. Marketing emails, if we ever send them, will be opt-in with one-tap unsubscribe.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers that make the app work, bound by their own confidentiality and data-protection obligations:

  • App stores & billing — Apple App Store, Google Play, and our subscription-management provider, to process and validate purchases.
  • Cloud infrastructure — hosting, databases, and authentication providers that store account and sync data.
  • Diagnostics & analytics — crash-reporting and product-analytics tools, configured without advertising identifiers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger or acquisition (in which case this policy continues to apply to data collected under it).

5. Retention

Account data and practice history are kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, associated personal data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons (kept only as long as required). Local-only practice data lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to processing of, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Exercise any of them by emailing support@fundedlot.com from the address linked to your account — we respond within the timelines your local law sets. Account deletion is also self-serve: see account & data deletion. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers are protected by recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted to what operating the Service requires, and authentication is handled by established identity providers. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

The Service is for adults preparing for trading evaluations and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Cookies & the website

fundedlot.com is a static informational site. It does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics to the website, it will be privacy-respecting and reflected here.

11. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. For material changes affecting app users, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@fundedlot.com.
