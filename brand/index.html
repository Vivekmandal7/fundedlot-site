Brand

One word: FundedLot

The name, the mark, the colors, and how to use them. Writing about us, reviewing the app, or telling a friend what to search for — everything you need is on this page.

The name

FundedLot — however you type it, it's us

Officially it's FundedLot: one word, capital F, capital L. In the wild we get searched as funded lot (two words), Fundedlot, fundedlot app, and occasionally "the funded lot simulator" — all of those mean this product. If you're writing about us, "FundedLot" is the form to use in text; the wordmark below styles the "lot" in volt lime.

Say / write

FundedLot — the prop-firm challenge practice app. Pronounced "funded lot," like a lot that is funded. One word in print, capital F and L.

What we're not called

Not "FundedLots," not "Funded Lots," not "FundLot." And since we're an educational simulator: never "FundedLot prop firm" — we aren't one, and we'd rather you say so.

Logo

The mark and the wordmark

A volt lime square (corner radius ~30%) carrying three ascending black candles. In dark contexts the mark may glow; in light contexts it casts a shadow. Download and use as-is — don't recolor, rotate, outline, or add effects.

Wordmark

"Funded" in bone (dark) or ink (light) + "lot" in the accent, set in Inter ExtraBold (800) with tight −0.045em tracking. Recreate it with the fonts below or request flat exports via press@.

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the height of one candle bar around the mark. Minimum size: 24px. On photography, use the mark on a solid volt or black chip.

Palette & type

Volt Noir and Ink & Lime

Two modes, one system. Dark is cinematic true black with a single volt light source; light is editorial bone paper with deep-lime ink. The accent never fills large background areas.

Typography

Inter + IBM Plex Mono

Headlines, UI and prose are set in Inter (400–800, optical sizing on) at large sizes with tight tracking. Every number, price, percentage and timestamp is set in IBM Plex Mono (400–600) — prose is never mono, numbers are never not. Both are open-source Google Fonts.

Questions

Brand FAQ

Is it "FundedLot" or "Funded Lot"?

One word: FundedLot. "Funded Lot," "Fundedlot" and "funded lot app" all refer to the same product and will all find us — but in writing, use FundedLot.

Can I use your screenshots and logo in a review or video?

Yes — editorial use in reviews, articles and videos is welcome, unmodified and with the app clearly identified as an educational simulator. For anything beyond editorial use, ask first via the press kit contact.

What may I not do with the brand?

Don't imply we're a prop firm, broker, or that we offer funded accounts or payouts; don't use the brand to promote signals or "guaranteed pass" services; don't recolor the mark or set the wordmark in another typeface.
