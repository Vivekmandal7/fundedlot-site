Account breach meaning in trading

Challenge rulesTrading glossary·Updated 24 October 2026
Definition. The automatic closure of a prop firm challenge or funded account after a hard rule, such as the daily loss limit or max drawdown, is broken.

In prop trading, an account breach means a hard rule was broken and the account is closed on the spot. The two usual causes are the daily loss limit and the max drawdown; firms also list rule violations such as banned strategies.

Quick answer

Account breach means the account hit a hard limit and is over. On a $100,000 challenge with a 5% daily limit, equity falling to $5,000 below the day's start, even in an open trade that later recovers, breaches it. The fee or the funded account is lost.

What causes a breach

CauseExample on $100,000
Daily loss limitEquity $5,000 below the day's start (5% limit)
Max drawdownEquity at $90,000 (10% static) or below a trailing floor
Rule violationProhibited strategy, copy trading between accounts, trading during restricted news

Example: the breach that recovers too late

You are down $3,000 on the day with a 5% limit. You open a trade that dips $2,100 against you before turning profitable. At the dip, equity was $5,100 below the day's start, so the account breached at that moment. The platform closes it automatically; the later recovery does not count. This is why limits are measured on equity, including floating P&L.

How to avoid a breach

  • Size every trade so one full stop uses at most a quarter of the daily limit.
  • Stop for the day after two losses; most breaches follow a revenge trade.
  • Know whether your max drawdown is static or trailing.
  • After a breach, an account reset is sold at a discount; fix the cause before buying it.

FAQ

What does account breached mean?

The prop firm closed the account because a hard rule, usually the daily loss limit or the max drawdown, was broken. The challenge fee or the funded account is lost.

Can a breached account be recovered?

No. Firms sell a reset or a new challenge instead, often at a discount.

Does a breach count open trades?

Yes, at most firms. Limits are checked on equity, so an open loss can breach the account before you close the trade.
