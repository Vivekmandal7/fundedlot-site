FAQ

Straight answers

If yours isn't here, email support@fundedlot.com — a human replies.

The product

What is FundedLot, in one paragraph?

A prop-firm challenge practice app. It's a realistic trading simulator that enforces real evaluation rules — profit targets, daily loss limits, drawdown, minimum trading days — on virtual funds, and coaches you by detecting every trading mistake and pricing it in dollars. You rehearse the challenge before paying a firm for a real attempt.

Is FundedLot a prop firm or broker?

Neither. We don't take deposits, route orders, offer real-money trading, sell funded accounts, or pay out profits. FundedLot is an educational simulator — the training ground, not the exam.

Will passing a FundedLot evaluation get me funded somewhere?

No. Passing earns a simulated funded stage inside the app and a shareable graduate certificate — recognition of practice, clearly labelled as simulation. No firm is obligated to honour it, and we promise no financial outcome. The real payoff is walking into a paid evaluation with the rules already boring to you.

How realistic is the simulation?

Prices are real market quotes across forex, metals, indices and crypto. Market hours are respected, spreads are realistic, and fills model slippage. Rules are evaluated against equity including open positions — the same basis real firms use — with timezone-aware daily resets. What no simulator can replicate is the feeling of real money; we're honest about that in our risk disclosure.

Which rules can I practice?

Profit targets, daily loss limits (balance or equity basis), static and trailing max drawdown, end-of-day variants, minimum trading days, consistency rules, one-step and two-step structures, and behavioural rules like stop-loss requirements. Profiles are editable, so you can mirror the fine print of the firm you're targeting. See the rules guide.

Does the coach tell me what to trade?

Never. It analyzes how you trade — sizing, stops, timing, streaks — and reports what each habit cost you. It will say "your average risk is 2.4% and it cost you $1,180 this month". It will not say "buy EURUSD". Nothing in the app is financial advice.

Money

What's actually free?

The full simulator, all instruments, real prices, the journal, coach headlines with mistake counts, and one complete evaluation. Free means free — no card, no expiring trial.

What do the paid tiers add?

Attempt packs ($4.99 / $9.99 / $24.99) add 1, 3 or 10 evaluations at any account size, one-time purchase. FundedLot Pro ($14.99/mo, $89.99/yr, or $199 lifetime) adds unlimited evaluations, full mistake-cost analysis, autopsies, the readiness score, and courses with the graduate certificate. See pricing.

How do I cancel? Can I get a refund?

Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; Pro stays active until the period ends. Refund requests go through the store you bought from, under their policies — we can't process card refunds directly because the stores handle all billing.

Why would I pay when broker demos are free?

A demo teaches you a platform. It cannot teach you rules it doesn't have: no demo breaches you for a 5% down day or tracks a trailing drawdown. The thing that fails ~90% of paid attempts is exactly the thing demos never simulate — that's the gap FundedLot exists for. More in this comparison.

Practical

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and Android phones (tablets work too). See the download page for store availability.

Do I need an account?

You can practice locally without one. Creating an account enables sync and backup features as they roll out. Deleting it is self-serve — see account deletion.

What data do you collect?

The short version: your email if you create an account, your practice activity inside the app, purchase records from the stores, and standard crash/usage analytics. No broker credentials, no financial documents, nothing sold to anyone. The long version is the privacy policy.

Is FundedLot affiliated with any prop firm?

No. We are independent. When our guides describe "typical" rules or fees, they describe common industry patterns, not any specific company's offer. Always read the actual rules of any firm you pay.

Is it "FundedLot", "Funded Lot", or "Fundedlot"?

One word, two capitals: FundedLot. People also find us by typing "funded lot", "fundedlot app", or "funded lot simulator" — all of those are this product. Details and logo files live on the brand page.

I found a bug / I have a feature idea.

We want it: support@fundedlot.com. Include your device model and what you expected to happen; screenshots make fixes faster.
