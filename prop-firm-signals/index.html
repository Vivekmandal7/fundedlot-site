Plain answers

Prop firm signal questions

Can I use signals on a prop firm challenge?

Most firms let you trade your own decisions from any analysis, and many forbid copy trading, account sharing or tools that trade for you. With FundedLot you read the signal and place the trade yourself, at your size, on your platform. Check your firm's rules on third-party signals before you start.

How do you stop a signal from breaking my daily loss limit?

The size, not the signal, decides that. The app caps your risk so one full stop uses at most a quarter of the daily loss limit and a tenth of the max loss, then rounds the lot size down. On a 5% daily limit that leaves room for four stops in a row before the limit.

My firm is not on the list. Does it work?

Yes. Choose "Other" in your plan and type the firm's daily loss, max loss and target. Personal and portfolio accounts work too, with your own risk per trade.

Do you promise I will pass?

No one honest can. What we offer is a money-back guarantee with published conditions: follow the plan for the guarantee period, take the signals at the size the app gives you, stay inside your firm's rules and do your monthly review. If you have not passed by then, ask for your money back in the app.

Where do I pay?

In the app, through the App Store or Google Play, monthly or yearly. The website holds your account and your plan only.
