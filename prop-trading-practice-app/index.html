Prop trading practice app

Practice is a habit. Habits live on your phone.

FundedLot is a prop trading practice app for iOS and Android. Ten focused minutes on the train beats a Saturday of theory — because what fails evaluations isn't knowledge, it's what you do 40 seconds after a losing trade.

What you actually practice

Not chart patterns. The four skills that decide evaluations.

Commonly cited industry estimates put first-attempt failure around 90% — and almost none of it is strategy. These are the trainable parts.

Sizing under a daily limit

Position size stops being a preference when 5% ends your day. You practice computing risk per trade against the room you have left — until the arithmetic is reflex, not spreadsheet work.

Behaviour after a loss

The revenge window — the minutes right after a red trade — is where most accounts die. The app detects the pattern in your own trading and shows you its dollar cost.

Living with a drawdown floor

Static and trailing floors change which trades are survivable. You learn to feel the distance to the floor the way you feel the edge of a cliff — by walking near it on virtual funds.

Patience across minimum days

Evaluations reward boring consistency over hero days. Practising the full multi-day structure teaches pacing no single session ever can.

A practice week

What a week in the app looks like

1

Days 1–2 · Calibrate

Start an evaluation with the rule profile of your target firm. Trade small. Goal: zero rule warnings, not profit. The risk guard tells you before any entry that could breach.

2

Days 3–5 · Push the target

Work toward the profit target at your planned risk per trade. Every close gets a process grade out of 100 — graded on discipline, not luck.

3

Weekend · Read the report

The Mistake Center ranks your errors by dollar cost. Pick the most expensive habit, set one rule against it, run the next evaluation on that rule.

Questions

Practice app FAQ

Which platforms does the practice app run on?

FundedLot is built for iOS and Android. See the download page for current store availability.

Do I need trading experience to start?

No. The glossary, courses and coach assume you're learning. If you can read a price chart, you can start an evaluation — the app explains every rule the moment it becomes relevant to your trade.

How is this different from a trading game?

Games reward streaks and dopamine. FundedLot enforces loss limits, grades your discipline, and ends the run when you breach — because that's what the real evaluation does. It's practice software, closer to a flight simulator than to an arcade.

Can I practise offline?

Live trading practice needs a connection for real market prices. Reports, lessons and your mistake history are available whenever you open the app.
