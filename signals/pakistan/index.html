Signals in Pakistan: questions

What time are signals in Pakistan?

Forex and gold signals come between 12:00 and 21:00 PKT, US index signals from 18:45 PKT (19:45 in New York winter), and crypto from 11:00 to 02:00 PKT.

Do prop firms accept traders from Pakistan?

Many do, but each firm keeps its own list of countries. Check the firm's terms before you buy a challenge; FundedLot works with any firm you choose.

Can I pay from Pakistan?

Yes, through Google Play or the App Store in your local currency where the store supports it. The website takes no payments.
