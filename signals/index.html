Plain answers

Signal questions

What does a FundedLot signal contain?

A market, a direction, an entry, a stop loss and three take-profit targets (TP1, TP2, TP3), with the reasoning in plain words. Updates follow when a target is reached or the stop moves. In the app, each signal also shows the lot size for your own account.

Which markets do the signals cover?

The AI scans 13 markets: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, gold, silver, the Dow, the Nasdaq 100, the S&P 500, Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana. The mentor's signals and your plan can take in any market worldwide, India and Japan included.

Which trading platforms can I use?

Any. MT4, MT5, cTrader, DXtrade, Match-Trader, TradeLocker, TradingView, NinjaTrader, Tradovate, or any broker or exchange. A signal is a set of prices you place yourself; FundedLot never connects to your trading account.

How is the lot size worked out?

From your account size and your risk per trade, divided by the distance from entry to stop. The app then caps it so one full stop never uses more than a quarter of your firm's daily loss limit or a tenth of its max loss, and rounds down, never up. The calculator on this page runs the same arithmetic.

Can I pay on the website?

No. The website is for your account and your plan. The subscription, FundedLot Mentorship, is bought in the app, monthly or yearly, through the App Store or Google Play, in your own currency. On a phone, every "Get the app" button on this site opens your store directly.

What is free?

Signing in and saving your plan, the public AI record on this site and in the app, the sizing calculator, free prop firm challenge practice on virtual funds, and the lessons. Live signals, the mentor, the AI coach and the full Mistake Lab come with the subscription.

Are the AI results real?

Yes. Each AI signal posts at the live price before the move, never after it, and a tracker follows it on 1-minute bars to a target, the stop or the time limit. Losses stay on the record next to the wins, and nothing is removed. A past record never promises future results.

Does my prop firm allow signals?

Most firms allow you to trade your own decisions from any analysis, and many forbid copy trading, shared accounts or trading tools run by someone else. With FundedLot you read the signal and place the trade yourself, at your own size. Check your firm's rules on third-party signals before you start.

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are educational market analysis. You decide whether to trade, where and at what size. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.
