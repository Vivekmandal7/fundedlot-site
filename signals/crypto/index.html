Sizing

The same signal, sized for your account

A signal says where. The size says how much, and that part belongs to your account: its balance, your firm's daily loss limit and max loss, your own risk per trade. The app works it out on every signal, and the website shows you the arithmetic.

Size a signal for your account

Worked example

A Bitcoin signal on a $10,000 account

Risk per trade1% = $100
Stop distance$900
Value of $1$1 per coin
Size$100 ÷ 900 = 0.11 BTC

A personal account sets its own risk. A prop account takes the firm's limits, and the app caps the risk so one stop never uses more than a quarter of the daily limit.

Plain answers

Crypto signal questions

Which coins do the signals cover?

Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana against the US dollar, scanned every 15 minutes between 06:00 and 21:00 UTC.

Do crypto signals have a stop loss?

Every one. Each signal carries an entry, a stop and three targets. When the first target is reached the stop moves to the entry, and after the second it moves to the first target.

Can I use them on Binance or another exchange?

Yes. A signal is a set of prices, so it works on any exchange, broker or prop firm that lists the coin. You place the orders yourself.

How has crypto done on the record?

The record shows every crypto signal with its result, from the first one on. The tally on this page counts them live.
