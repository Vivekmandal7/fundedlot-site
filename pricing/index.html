Pricing questions

Why is the price not on this page?

The App Store and Google Play set the price in your own currency, and the app shows it before you confirm. Showing one dollar figure here would be wrong for most of the world.

Can I pay on the website?

No. Sign in and save your plan here, then join in the app. On an Android phone the button opens Google Play; on an iPhone it opens the App Store.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; access runs to the end of the paid period. Store refunds follow the store's policy, and the money-back guarantee has its own published conditions, asked for in the app. See our terms of service.

Is there a free trial?

No trial. The free tier stays free for good, the public AI record shows how the signals did before you pay, and the money-back guarantee covers the membership.

Am I buying a funded account?

No. FundedLot sells educational signals, mentorship and tools. It is not a broker or a prop firm, holds no money for you and offers no funded accounts or payouts. Practice challenges in the app use virtual funds.
